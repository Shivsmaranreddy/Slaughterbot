# Problem and proposed solution

## Research question

Can a small modular system connect operational records with transparent AI experiments?

Customer and MSME needs are hypotheses until user research confirms them.

## Proposed workflow

Create a fictional customer order and follow its status.

Record batch input, output and waste with mass-balance checks.

Review experimental model evidence and missing-model abstentions.

## Existing solutions

Marel documents commercial processing control and traceability.

This educational project does not claim industrial equivalence.

Compare actual workflows, integration effort and costs before claiming an advantage.
