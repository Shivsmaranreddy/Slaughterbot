# Market, customer and MSME study plan
The proposed customer needs are understandable order status and transparent service information.
The proposed MSME needs are batch records, waste accounting, maintenance and task visibility.
These are hypotheses. Interview users with consent, avoid personal records and document actual
workflows before prioritizing product features or proposing a price.

## SWOT
Strengths: modular files, visible limitations, inexpensive software demonstration.
Weaknesses: untrained models, limited simulator, no production access controls.
Opportunities: education, recordkeeping pilots and reproducible comparison experiments.
Threats: misleading safety interpretation, unsuitable data, maintenance burden and poor adoption.

## PESTEL questions
Political: which school/local authorities must approve a demonstration?
Economic: what budget and ongoing maintenance cost can users support?
Social: are users comfortable with the project framing and AI uncertainty?
Technological: what power, connectivity and equipment are actually available?
Environmental: can waste metrics be measured consistently rather than guessed?
Legal: which data permissions and operational requirements apply to the intended deployment?
These are investigation questions, not legal or market findings.

EDGE is a future local configuration. ENTERPRISE is a future multi-location product concept.
Subscription tiers and sub-2-lakh hardware are unvalidated targets, not quoted offers.
