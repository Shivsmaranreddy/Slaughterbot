# Existing solutions

## Commercial processing and traceability software
Marel's beef-processing material describes Innova primary-processing control and traceability.
JBT Marel's quality-control brochure describes linking quality-control data with traceability.
These provide existing examples of production-management functionality; this student project
does not claim to surpass their industrial validation, integration or support.

## Established computer vision and reinforcement-learning tools
PyTorch documents transfer learning from pretrained networks. Gymnasium documents standard
environment reset/step interfaces. The project uses established methods rather than presenting
framework capabilities as original inventions.

## Position of this project
This is an educational, modular implementation joining notebooks, a local management demo and
an inert-object simulator. Potential accessibility to a school or small business is a design goal,
not a measured commercial advantage. The combination itself is not evidence of patent novelty.

## Research still needed
Interview potential users with permission. Compare actual inventory workflows, integration effort,
training needs and total ownership costs. Investigate visual-assessment and ultrasound publications
using matching tasks and data; do not compare unrelated published accuracies. Verify dataset rights
at source before acquisition. Document alternatives fairly and separate vendor statements from tests.
