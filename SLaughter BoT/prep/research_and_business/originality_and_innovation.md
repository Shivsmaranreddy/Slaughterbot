# Originality and innovation
Claim: independently implement and explain a transparent integration of model training, provenance,
operational records and a deterministic safety boundary. Validate this claim with a development log,
source history and repeatable experiments. Credit frameworks and source material.

Hypothesis: learned scenario selection may improve policy robustness compared with narrow training.
The paired evaluation is designed to test it; improvement is not assumed.

No claim: first-ever AI slaughterhouse, autonomous slaughtering, certified spoilage detection,
biologically accurate ultrasound reconstruction, or proven commercial superiority.
